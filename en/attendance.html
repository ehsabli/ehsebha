How is attendance calculated?

The absence rate equals absences divided by total classes times 100, and the attendance rate is the rest up to 100. Example: 3 absences out of 40 is a 7.5% absence rate and 92.5% attendance. Many schools and universities require a minimum attendance such as 75%, so keep an eye on it.
