Calculator Needed grade

Set the percentage you are aiming for and your current scores to see how many of the remaining points you need.

How many points do you need?

How do you find the needed grade?

Multiply the target percentage by the total score and divide by 100, then subtract your current score. Example: targeting 80% of 100 with 25 points already, you need 80 − 25 = 55 points from the remaining ones. If the result is more than the points left the target is not possible; if it is zero or less you have already reached it.
