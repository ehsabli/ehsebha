How do fraction operations work?

For addition and subtraction we make the denominators equal, then add the numerators. For multiplication we multiply numerator by numerator and denominator by denominator. For division we flip the second fraction and multiply. Example: 1/2 + 3/4 = 2/4 + 3/4 = 5/4. The calculator simplifies the result automatically by dividing both parts by their greatest common divisor.
