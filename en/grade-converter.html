Convert your grade

How does a grade convert between scales?

The conversion keeps the same ratio: divide your score by the original scale, then multiply by the new scale. Example: 17 out of 20 equals (17 / 20) x 100 = 85 out of 100. This helps when comparing different school or university systems.
