How is ad income calculated?

Ad networks pay per thousand ad impressions, called CPM. Earnings = page views divided by 1000, times the CPM. The CPM depends on the visitors' countries, the ad format, the season and the site topic, and it changes every day. Treat every number here as a rough estimate, never a guarantee.
